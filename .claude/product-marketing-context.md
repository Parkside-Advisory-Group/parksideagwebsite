## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Parkside Advisory Group helps businesses turn missed follow-up, manual reporting, and unclear handoffs into practical AI-supported operating systems.
**What it does:** Consulting firm that inspects one recurring business workflow, identifies where work leaks (no owner, no follow-up, manual rework), and designs/builds the first practical AI or automation system — with human review points kept explicit.
**Product category:** AI workflow automation consulting / AI operations consulting (customers search: "AI consultant", "workflow automation for small business", "automate follow-up").
**Product type:** Professional services with a productized ladder.
**Business model:** Free lead magnet → paid engagements. Ladder: (1) Free AI Workflow Savings Snapshot, (2) paid AI Operations Blueprint, (3) Workflow Automation Sprint / AI Agent Build, (4) AI Operations Retainer. **Never publish fixed pricing or guaranteed savings** (hard rule from AGENTS.md).

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** "Most businesses do not have an AI problem. They have a handoff problem." Work is dropped between owners — new leads sit, estimates age out, reporting is rebuilt weekly.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- AI tools get tested once, then abandoned because no workflow owns them
- Agencies sell broad transformation projects instead of fixing one workflow
- A better tool will not fix an unclear next step
**What it costs them:** Lost revenue from cold estimates, hours of manual reporting, owner attention consumed by status-chasing.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of buying AI hype; embarrassment that the process lives in someone's head; fatigue from chasing the same work every week.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** AI/automation consultancies and agencies — fall short by leading with tools and transformation scope instead of the workflow.
**Secondary:** Off-the-shelf SaaS (CRMs, chatbots, Zapier-style automation) — falls short because nobody defines the owner, trigger, and review point; tools get orphaned.
**Indirect:** Hiring another admin/coordinator, or doing nothing — falls short because the process stays undocumented and memory-dependent.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Starts with a free diagnostic (Snapshot) on ONE workflow, not a pitch for a program
- Founder-level project-controls discipline from $500M+ utility/energy infrastructure programs applied to everyday business workflows
- Explicit guardrails: names what should NOT be automated; keeps human review points
- Will say "this workflow is not ready" and name what to fix first
**How we do it differently:** Inspect the work → find the first build → install the operating layer → put owners on every step → tune from real use.
**Why customers choose us:** A useful answer before spending money; discipline without hype.

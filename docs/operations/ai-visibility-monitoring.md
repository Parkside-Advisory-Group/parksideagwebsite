# AI Visibility Monitoring — Parkside Advisory Group

Monthly check of whether parksideag.com is cited or mentioned in AI-generated answers
(ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Copilot). Log results in the table
at the bottom; compare month over month.

## How to run a check

For each query below, ask it in ChatGPT (with search), Perplexity, and Google
(watch for the AI Overview). Record:

1. Did an AI answer appear?
2. Was Parkside / parksideag.com cited or mentioned?
3. Who WAS cited? (competitors, directories, publishers)
4. Which page of ours got cited, if any?

## When Parkside is NOT cited

Note who was, then check their page: structure (FAQ? tables? dated?), authority
(stats, author), and whether the gap suggests a new field note or a page upgrade.
Content lever priority (Princeton GEO study): cite sources (+40%), statistics (+37%),
expert quotes (+30%), clarity (+20%).
